# A1-B007X — Discovery-to-Compact Consolidation Gate

**Placement:** immediately after A1-B007 PASS.

## Objective

Test whether APC can use an overcomplete temporary discovery system and then retain the discovered computation in a much smaller persistent primitive.

## Sequence

```text
B007 recurrence PASS
      ↓
metric/shadow audit
      ↓
novel-task calibration
      ↓
temporary capacity ladder
      ↓
compact direct-learning control
      ↓
overcomplete -> compact distillation
      ↓
shadow validation
      ↓
persistent installation
      ↓
100% temp release
      ↓
fresh-runtime recurrence
      ↓
capacity-gap verdict
```

## Stop gates

- X-G0: B007 recurrence passed.
- X-G1: existing bank/composition fails novel task.
- X-G2: at least one overcomplete tier solves it robustly.
- X-G3: candidate retains >=95% of temp performance.
- X-G4: shadow safety passes.
- X-G5: persistent-only recurrence passes.

## Negative results

If compact direct learning is equally effective, report:

> compressibility may hold, but no discovery-capacity advantage is demonstrated.

Do not keep increasing size until the desired result appears.

## Downstream blocking

Keep learned retrieval/router/novelty/full-loop work blocked until this gate's audit is complete, unless the user explicitly approves otherwise.
