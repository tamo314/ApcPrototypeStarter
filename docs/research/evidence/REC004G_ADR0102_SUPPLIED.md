**### Context**

\`B-C005REC-004E\`/ADR-0100 observed that REC-004D's P/I01-I05 training curves had not plateaued by step=6000 at any matched same-LR-phase checkpoint pair (\`OPTIMIZATION\_PROGRESS\_OBSERVED\`), and \`B-C005REC-004F\`/ADR-0101's oracle-attention probe additionally showed the length-10 residual was heterogeneous across inits rather than a single shared mechanism. Neither task's own charter authorized further training. The user then directly instructed, in chat, a specific limited experiment: resume each of REC-004D's 5 saved P training states from their complete step=6000 checkpoint (weights + AdamW optimizer state + \`CosineAnnealingLR\` scheduler state + CPU/CUDA RNG state) and continue training 6000 more updates each (to a new cumulative 12000), under the exact same recipe -- testing the under-convergence hypothesis for the already-effective P, not re-testing whether position bias helps at all. The user explicitly noted 12000 is a new proposed value, not a previously-approved budget, and that U does not need to be retrained (comparison is each P against its own step=6000 state). This instruction was formalized as \`docs/CODEX\_TASKS\_PHASE\_B\_B2\_MIRROR\_BUDGET\_EXTENSION\_12000.md\` (task B-C005REC-004G, deliberately NOT \`B-C005REC-005\` -- see Status above) before implementation, mirroring the written-contract discipline of every prior REC task, and fixes as an explicit, unconditional charter constraint that even a unanimous floor pass at step=12000 does not, by itself, select a candidate, build a child bundle, or run an RG3 recheck -- those remain gated behind a separate future instruction.

**### Evidence**

1\. **\*\*Resume mechanics are bit-exact, not approximate.\*\*** REC-004D's saved \`training\_states/step6000.pt\` per init contains the complete state needed for gapless continuation (\`primitive\_state\_dict\`, \`optimizer\_state\_dict\`, \`scheduler\_state\_dict\` with \`CosineAnnealingLR\`'s \`last\_epoch=6000\`/\`base\_lrs\`, \`cpu\_rng\_state\`, \`cuda\_rng\_state\`). A standalone CPU proof (and the module's own \`test\_load\_state\_dict\_resume\_reproduces\_uninterrupted\_lr\_trajectory\`) confirms that constructing fresh \`AdamW\`/\`CosineAnnealingLR\` objects, calling \`optimizer.load\_state\_dict(...)\` then \`scheduler.load\_state\_dict(...)\` in that order, and continuing \`.step()\` reproduces the exact LR trajectory an uninterrupted run would have produced -- not a coincidence of REC-004D's particular schedule phase, but a general property of this resume order (\`optimizer.load\_state\_dict\` restores \`param\_groups[0]['lr']\`; the scheduler's own harmless initial no-op step at construction never overwrites it; \`scheduler.load\_state\_dict\` then restores only the scheduler's internal counters). Per-step training data is regenerated via \`incremental\_budget\_calibration.\_generate\_step\_training\_examples(seed, step, ...)\`, a pure function of \`(seed, step)\` using its own local \`random.Random\` (never the ambient RNG stream), so steps 6001-12000 are freshly, deterministically generated -- never a silent repeat of steps 1-6000.

2\. **\*\*Source replay verifies all 5 resumed states before any new optimizer update runs.\*\*** For each init, the loaded \`primitive\_state\_dict\`'s canonical hash is checked against REC-004D's own \`learning\_curve.jsonl\` row for \`(init\_id, arm=P, step=6000)\`, then a plain forward on the resumed weights is evaluated on the same 1024-example existing-validation split and compared to REC-004D's own recorded EM (\`source\_replay.json\`: all 5 inits \`status: VERIFIED\`, hash and EM match exactly, tolerance \`1e-9\`). Any mismatch would have set \`implementation\_status: BLOCKED\` before training started, for every init, not just the affected one.

3\. **\*\*All 5 inits improve substantially and consistently; none diverge\*\*** (\`cost\_accounting.json\`: exactly 30000 new optimizer updates, 6000 per init, 0 skipped; \`diverged\_inits: {}\`). Existing-validation EM, step=6000 -> step=12000 (\`paired\_extension\_comparison.json\` / \`summary.json\`):

   | Init | EM @ 6000 | EM @ 12000 | Delta | Clears 0.95? |

   |---|---:|---:|---:|---|

   | I01 | 0.6768 | 0.8662 | +0.1895 | no |

   | I02 | 0.7070 | 0.9336 | +0.2266 | no |

   | I03 | 0.5713 | 0.8037 | +0.2324 | no |

   | I04 | 0.7373 | 0.9688 | +0.2314 | **\*\*yes\*\*** |

   | I05 | 0.9023 | 0.9766 | +0.0742 | **\*\*yes\*\*** |

   Mean EM rises from 0.719 (REC-004D's own reported mean) to 0.910. 2 of 5 (I04, I05) now clear the pre-registered 0.95 floor; I02 comes close (0.934) but does not; I01 and I03 remain further below (0.866, 0.804).

4\. **\*\*The gain concentrates specifically at length 10, exactly the length REC-004D/E identified as the residual, and is dramatic for every init including the two that still fail the floor\*\*** (\`paired\_extension\_comparison.json\`, by-length breakdown, length-10 correct/206): I01 6->88, I02 40->157, I03 10->26, I04 95->203, I05 153->201. I03's length-10 gain (+2.6x, still only 12.6%) is markedly smaller than the other four's, consistent with I03 remaining the weakest init overall at step=12000 -- the under-convergence effect is real and broad but not uniform in magnitude.

5\. **\*\*The bias term remains load-bearing at step=12000, exactly as at step=6000\*\*** -- zeroing \`position\_bias\_out.weight\` on each trained P collapses existing-validation EM to exactly 0.0 for all 5 inits, and restoring it reproduces the original step=12000 predictions exactly (\`bias\_ablation.json\`: \`restore\_reproduces\_original\_predictions\_exactly: true\` for all 5).

6\. **\*\*Non-mutation, freeze, and side-effect audits pass exactly as in every prior REC task.\*\*** Core's canonical hash, all 12 protected primitives' hashes, and REC-004A's 3 fixed-candidate hashes are byte-identical before/after; REC-004D's own \`run\_001/learning\_curve.jsonl\` and all 5 \`training\_states/step6000.pt\` source files are confirmed byte-identical (raw sha256) before/after -- this task only ever reads them (\`freeze\_audit.json\`: \`core\_unchanged\`/\`protected\_operations\_unchanged\`/\`fixed\_candidates\_unchanged\`/\`rec004d\_run\_001\_source\_files\_unchanged\` all \`true\`). All 5 tracked shared-cache paths are byte-identical before/after (\`side\_effect\_audit.json\`). Total wall-clock for all 30000 updates plus periodic evaluation was 212s (\`cost\_accounting.json\`).

7\. **\*\*Test suite.\*\*** \`tests/test\_mirror\_budget\_extension.py\` (19 CPU-only tests: constant reuse from REC-004D rather than retyping; a source-scan proof the module never imports or dispatches REC-004F's oracle-attention substitution or any later task; \`load\_source\_training\_state\`/\`verify\_source\_checkpoint\_hash\` correctness and mismatch detection against a synthetic "REC-004D-shaped" fixture built fresh in \`tmp\_path\` (never against the real, large \`rec004d/run\_001\` tree); CPU RNG restore determinism; the isolated optimizer/scheduler resume-continuity proof; an end-to-end tiny real-CPU-training run through \`run\_source\_replay\`+\`run\_one\_extension\` confirming checkpoint steps, \`bias\_ablation\` presence at the target step, and zero divergence; \`build\_candidate\_decision\`'s floor logic for pass/fail/unanimous-pass, in every case confirming \`selected\_init\`/\`child\_bundle\` stay \`null\` and \`rg3\_recheck\` stays \`NOT\_EXECUTED\`; the dynamic guard blocking execution under \`frozen\_evaluation()\`; and dispatcher config-loader tests mirroring REC-004D's own) all pass. \`python -m ruff check .\` and \`python -m mypy src/apc\` (139 source files, up from ADR-0101's 138) are clean repo-wide. Full-repo \`python -m pytest -q\`: \`2197 passed, 4 warnings in 1413.12s (0:23:33)\` -- zero failures, up from ADR-0101's \`2178 passed\` by exactly this task's 19 new tests; all 4 warnings are the same pre-existing, unrelated \`torch\` nested-tensor prototype-API warning and LR-trace-preflight ordering warning noted in every prior REC ADR.

**### Consequences**

\- **\*\*No candidate was selected, no child bundle was built, and no RG3 recheck ran\*\*** -- \`selected\_init: null\`, \`selected\_intervention: null\`, \`child\_bundle: null\`, \`rg3\_recheck: NOT\_EXECUTED\`, unconditionally, per this task's own charter. This holds even though 2 of 5 inits (I04, I05) individually cleared the 0.95 floor at step=12000: REC-004D's own all-five, no-best-of-five adoption rule is unchanged, and this task's own charter additionally required a separate future instruction before any candidate-adoption step could run even in a unanimous-pass scenario.

\- **\*\*The under-convergence hypothesis is strongly confirmed as a real, broad effect, not a coincidence of any one init.\*\*** All 5 inits gained +0.07 to +0.23 EM from 6000 more updates under an unchanged recipe, concentrated at length 10 -- REC-004E's \`OPTIMIZATION\_PROGRESS\_OBSERVED\` finding was not a false lead. However, the effect size is heterogeneous (I03 gains the least in absolute length-10 terms and remains the weakest init), so a future task should not assume a further budget increase alone would bring the remaining 3 inits (I01, I02, I03) over the floor without evidence from a further, separately-authorized experiment.

\- **\*\*No historical PASS/FAIL is changed.\*\*** \`B-C005REC-001\` through \`-004F\` (ADR-0092-0101) are preserved as-is; this task extends training on copies of REC-004D's own checkpoints and published no new bundle.

\- **\*\*No shared cache file was written, and no REC-004D artifact was modified\*\*** (Evidence 6). All new files are under \`src/\`, \`tests/\`, \`scripts/\`, \`configs/\`, \`docs/\`, and this task's own \`runs/phase\_b\_b2\_model\_bundle\_recovery/rec004g/run\_001/\` namespace; \`runs/phase\_b\_b2\_model\_bundle\_recovery/rec004d/\` was read-only throughout.

\- Per the user's own explicit-authorization scope, only \`B-C005REC-004G\` was executed; \`B-C005REC-005\` (the real five-model cohort, unaffected by this task) onward, \`B-C005R3-011\`, \`B-C006\`, and Task Inference remain blocked pending an explicit next user instruction. Whether to extend the budget further for I01/I02/I03, investigate why I03 lags the other four, or otherwise respond to this result is not decided here.
